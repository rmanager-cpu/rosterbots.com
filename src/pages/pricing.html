Pricing questions

Why is it half price after Week 8?

Because half the season is left. The weekly price is the same either way, about $1.67 a week for Auto Protect and about $2.78 a week for Agentic Protection.

What does Agentic Protection never do?

It never makes a trade, never claims waivers and never spends FAAB. It never drops a starter who's playing, a player who'll be back from injury, or a player most ESPN leagues roster. When it isn't sure, it asks you.

Is the free version really free?

Yes. One-Tap Alerts tell you about every problem and put the fix one tap away. Automatic moves are what the season passes pay for.

Which fantasy hosts does it work with?

ESPN fantasy football leagues. We'll add more hosts when we can do it properly.

When can I buy a pass?

When RosterBots opens on your phone. Join the waitlist and we'll email you first.
