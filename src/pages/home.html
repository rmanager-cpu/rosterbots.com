Keep it safe

RosterBots only acts within these rules.

Every rule is written into the code, not left to judgment. Every move is checked against ESPN before and after it's made.

  • Only acts on starters who are officially out, on IR, suspended, on bye or projected for zero.
  • Never benches a starter who is playing. Questionable and doubtful players are always your call.
  • Never makes a trade, claims waivers or spends FAAB.
  • Only drops a player you've marked Droppable, and never one who'll be back.
  • A move only counts once a fresh read from ESPN shows it, and you get a receipt every time.
  • Never sees your ESPN password. The AI never sees your sign-in or your email.
