Account deletion

Delete your RosterBots account

RosterBots: Hangover Protection, by Turbo Devs. Android package com.rosterbots.app.

Deleting your account permanently removes everything RosterBots stored about you: every league you were watching, your ESPN sign-in, your settings, your activity and receipts, and your phone's notification token. Your ESPN account itself is never touched. This can't be undone.

If you have the app

Open RosterBots, go to Settings, and tap Delete my account under Account. It deletes everything right away and signs you out. You don't need us for this.

If you don't have the app, or can't open it

Email support@rosterbots.com with the subject "Delete my RosterBots account". Send it from the Google account you signed in with, if you signed in, and include whatever you can of:

  • The email address on your RosterBots account, if it has one
  • Your ESPN league name and team name, as the app's Home screen showed them
  • Roughly when you started using RosterBots

We'll delete the account and confirm by email when it's done.

What gets deleted

Your account record, your encrypted ESPN sign-in, every league connection and its settings, the lineup activity and receipts RosterBots kept, and any notification token for your phones. If you joined a waitlist and want off it too, say so in the email. See the privacy policy for everything we store.
