MegaCast. Your league, on the air.

A broadcast booth for your fantasy league. Three announcers call your draft live in the room, pick by pick, and come back every week with a recap episode about your league: who won, who blew it, and who should never be trusted with a lineup again.

Draft night, called live

The booth calls every pick as it lands, with your league's own running jokes, on the TV and the speaker in the room.

A weekly show about your league

A few minutes of recap every week, built from your league's real scores, so the trash talk has receipts.

Your league's lore

You tell the booth who the defending champion is and who can't set a lineup. It only says what's in your league's data and what you've told it.
